# Shipping Policy (sample document)

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

Orders over 50 USD qualify for free standard shipping.

International orders ship to selected countries and take 7 to 14 business days. Customers are responsible for customs duties.

Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days. Orders placed on weekends are processed on the next business day.
