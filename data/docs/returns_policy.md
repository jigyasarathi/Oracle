# Returns and Refunds Policy (sample document)

Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received at the distribution center.

Final-sale items cannot be returned. Gift cards cannot be refunded.

Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items. For all other returns, the customer pays return shipping.
