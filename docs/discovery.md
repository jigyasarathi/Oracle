# Discovery notes (template)

Fill this in for an imaginary or real customer before building more features.

- **Customer / industry:**
- **Who asks the questions?** (roles)
- **Questions they ask today** (collect 20 real examples; these become your eval set):
- **Data sources:** (warehouse tables, documents, owners, freshness)
- **Data readiness issues:** (missing definitions, duplicates, permissions)
- **Risk / compliance constraints:** (PII, read-only, audit)
- **Success criteria:** (e.g. 90% answer accuracy, p95 latency under 20 s, cost per question under X)
